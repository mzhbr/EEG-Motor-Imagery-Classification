EEG Motor Imagery Classification - Experimental Results

Dataset:
PhysioNet EEG Motor Movement/Imagery Dataset (EEGMMIDB)

Subjects:
1-10

Runs:
4, 8, 12

Total trials:
450

Channels:
64

Sampling frequency:
160 Hz

Epoch length:
4 seconds

Left-hand motor imagery
Right-hand motor imagery
